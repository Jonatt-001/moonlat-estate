About MoonLat

A better way to find property.

MoonLat is a Nigerian property discovery marketplace built to make finding homes, apartments, shortlets, land and commercial spaces across Nigeria simpler, clearer and more dependable.

Explore Properties Our Mission
Why MoonLat

Property search should feel less complicated.

Finding property in Nigeria can involve scattered listings, incomplete information, unclear pricing, multiple phone calls and uncertainty about who is actually behind a listing.

MoonLat is designed around a different experience: bring property discovery, useful listing information and trusted marketplace signals into one place.

Whether someone is looking for a new home, a rental, a shortlet, an investment opportunity or commercial space, MoonLat is built to help them move from searching to making an informed decision.
