What's the best strategy for first-time buyers in this competitive market?

I've been helping several first-time buyers lately and the market is incredibly competitive. Pre-approval is essential, but what other strategies are working for you?

Here are a few things I've been recommending:

1. Escalation clauses - Shows sellers you're serious and willing to beat other offers up to a certain limit.
2. Flexible closing dates - Sometimes accommodating the seller's timeline can make your offer stand out even if it's not the highest.
3. Personal letters - In some markets, a heartfelt letter to the seller can still make a difference.

What strategies have worked for you recently? Let's share insights with the community!
